# Mode Style Guides

Use this as a reference when authoring headline/impact/action/watch fields per mode. Keep severity within {Opportunity, Watch, High Risk, Info}.

## Standard
- Voice: clear, calm, practical; neutral/advisory.
- Severity: use Opportunity for supportive aspects (trine, sextile, quintile), Watch for friction (square, semisquare, quincunx), High Risk only for intense triggers (hard Mars/Pluto/Saturn angles).
- Patterns:
	- Headline: short benefit/risk statement.
	- Impact: what area or theme is affected.
	- Action: one concrete step to leverage or mitigate.

## Business
- Voice: concise, strategic, impact-focused; risk/ROI framing.
- Severity: Opportunity for flow/creativity (trine, sextile, quintile), Watch for execution risk (square, semisquare, quincunx), High Risk for structural or reputational threats.
- Patterns:
	- Headline: business outcome (growth, risk, alignment).
	- Impact: teams, revenue, ops, stakeholders.
	- Action: decision, mitigation, or alignment step.

## Space Force
- Voice: operational, mission/flight discipline, security posture.
- Severity: Watch for navigational/coordination tension; High Risk for collision of mandates or security posture shifts; Opportunity for harmonics that aid coordination.
- Patterns:
	- Headline: mission/ops framing.
	- Impact: comms, coordination, readiness, security.
	- Action: checklist item, comms protocol, risk review.

## Raves
- Voice: expressive, vibe-forward, social/creative energy.
- Severity: Opportunity for flow/creative mixes; Watch for chaotic/frictive mixes; High Risk rarely (only for volatile, crowd/energy clashes).
- Patterns:
	- Headline: vibe or emotional cue.
	- Impact: social dynamics, mood, creative surge.
	- Action: channel/curate energy, pacing, boundaries.
