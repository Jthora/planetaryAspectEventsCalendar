# Raves Planet Themes

## Canonical Themes
- Sun: headline energy / mainstage draw
- Moon: vibe and mood flux
- Mercury: comms, meetups, wayfinding, group chats
- Venus: outfit/aesthetic cohesion, social ease
- Mars: mosh/kinetic intensity, edge and risk
- Jupiter: scale, guest-list hype, expansion of crowd
- Saturn: security, lines, rules, capacity constraints
- Uranus: surprises, genre flips, sudden drops
- Neptune: trance/dissociation, dreamy flow, escapism
- Pluto: deep intensity, catharsis, bass weight
- North Node: emerging scenes and future flavor
- South Node: nostalgia sets and throwback vibes
- Chiron: safe/healing spaces, consent, recovery

## Notes
- Keep themes user-agnostic (no personal guarantees)
- Ensure every theme is present to avoid empty defaults
- Themes should harmonize with music/vibe cues (e.g., Mars pairs with higher edge/energy genres; Venus with aesthetic/outfit-friendly vibes)
