# Raves Sample Scenarios (Sanity Prompts)

Use these to spot-check outputs once guidance is populated.

- High-hype safe night: strong Opportunity, orderly, social-friendly, house/techno fit, bring friends ok, outfit bold.
- Chaotic mosh risk: High Risk or Watch with Mars/Saturn stress; warn for crowd surges, conflict potential, keep crew close.
- Chill ambient float: Info/Watch with Neptune/Water tones; solo friendly, trance/downtempo fit, soft outfit guidance.
- Social mixer: Air/Gemini/Libra cues; highlight meetups, friend-making green light, light conflict risk.
- Solo journey: Emphasize self-guided flow, safe navigation, choose smaller stages, avoid over-stimulation if risky.
- Genre flip night: Uranus emphasis; expect surprises, flexible plans, pack versatile outfit.
- Security/constraint heavy: Saturn emphasis; strict entry, long lines, hydrate/plan buffer, less spontaneity.
- Nostalgia/throwback: South Node; expect classics, familiar crowd vibe, dress retro.
- Healing/consent focus: Chiron; safe spaces, gentle vibe, highlight consent and pacing.
- Extras examples to spot: music_genre/subgenre/theme/style/speed/tone/vibe populated; outfit_cue; social_mode (solo/crew); friend_making_risk; chaos_order; safety_flag; conflict_risk; crowd_profile when relevant.
